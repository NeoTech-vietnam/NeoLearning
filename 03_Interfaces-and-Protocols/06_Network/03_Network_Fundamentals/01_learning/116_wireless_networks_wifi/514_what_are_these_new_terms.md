#### Wi-Fi terms
- In Wi-Fi, we do not have some normal terms like: 5G, 4G or 3G, instead, we have Wi-Fi 6, Wi-Fi 5, and so on. These are the new terms that are used to describe the different generations of Wi-Fi technology.
- Well all know that 5G is better than 4G, and 4G is better than 3G, which means the users can just see in the number and believe that the higher number is better than the lower number
- Wi-Fi has some weird terms:

| Amendment           | Release Year    | Max Speed (Mbps) | Frequency Bands (GHz) | Key Features                                 |
