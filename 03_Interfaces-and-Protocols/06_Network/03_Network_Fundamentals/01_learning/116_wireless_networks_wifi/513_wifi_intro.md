### Notes Section (Main Notes)

#### How important is Wi-Fi in today's world?
- Wi-Fi is essential for us today, we expect to have Wi-Fi everywhere, in our homes, offices, cafes, airports, and even in public spaces. It has become a fundamental part of our daily lives, enabling us to connect to the internet and access information, communicate with others, and perform various tasks online.

#### Comparsion between Wi-Fi and Ethernet (Cable)

| Feature              | Cable (Ethernet)                                                                            | Wi-Fi (Wireless)                                                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Connection Type**  | Physical cable                                                                              | Wireless radio signals                                                                      |
| **Speed**            | Very high and consistent (e.g., 1 Gbps+)                                                    | Varies; can be high but inconsistent (depends on standard like Wi-Fi 6)                     |
| **Stability**        | Very stable                                                                                 | Can fluctuate due to interference                                                           |
| **Latency (Ping)**   | Low and consistent                                                                          | Higher and variable                                                                         |
| **Reliability**      | Not affected by environment                                                                 | Affected by walls, distance, interference                                                   |
| **Distance / Range** | Typically up to **100 meters** per Ethernet cable (standard Cat5e/Cat6) without signal loss | Typically **20–50 meters indoors**, up to ~100 meters outdoors (reduced by walls/obstacles) |
| **Mobility**         | Limited (must stay connected by cable)                                                      | High mobility within coverage area                                                          |
| **Setup Difficulty** | Requires cables and ports                                                                   | Easy to set up                                                                              |
| **Security**         | More secure (physical access needed)                                                        | Less secure but can be protected (e.g., WPA3)                                               |
| **Cost**             | Cable + installation cost                                                                   | Usually included with router                                                                |
| **Best Use Cases**   | Gaming, servers, industrial control, robotics                                               | Mobile devices, general browsing, smart homes                                               |
| **Interference**     | None                                                                                        | Possible (other networks, devices)                                                          |

#### Wi-Fi properties
- Wi-Fi uses a different range of frequencies, typically in the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands, to transmit data wirelessly. 
- It uses radio waves to communicate between devices and a wireless router or access point. The router acts as a central hub that connects devices to the internet and allows them to communicate with each other.
- 5 GHz Wi-Fi offers faster speeds and less interference but has a shorter range compared to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, which has a longer range but can be slower and more prone to interference.
  - It is also absorbed more easily by walls and obstacles, which can reduce its effective range.

#### Wireless Issues
- Privacy concerns: If you listen the same frequency, you can capture the data that is being transmitted over the Wi-Fi network, which can lead to privacy breaches and security risks.
- However, with proper security measures such as encryption (e.g., WPA3) and strong passwords, you can mitigate these risks and protect your Wi-Fi network from unauthorized access.

#### Directional attenna
- A directional antenna is a type of antenna that focuses the radio signal in a specific direction, rather than broadcasting it in all directions like an omnidirectional antenna. 
- This can help to improve the signal strength and range in a specific area, while reducing interference from other devices and networks. 
- Directional antennas are commonly used in Wi-Fi networks to enhance coverage and performance in specific areas, such as long-distance point-to-point connections or to provide better coverage in a specific direction.

#### Omnidirectional attenna
- An omnidirectional antenna is a type of antenna that radiates radio signals in all directions equally. It is designed to provide coverage in a 360-degree pattern, allowing devices to connect from any direction within its range. 
- Omnidirectional antennas are commonly used in Wi-Fi networks to provide broad coverage in areas where devices may be located in various directions, such as in homes, offices, and public spaces. 
- They are ideal for general use and can support multiple devices connecting from different angles.
