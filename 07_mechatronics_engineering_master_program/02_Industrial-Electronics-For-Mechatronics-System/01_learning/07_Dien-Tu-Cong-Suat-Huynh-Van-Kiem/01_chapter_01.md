### Notes Section (Main Notes)

#### definitions

There are some names of this courses:
- Power Electronics
- Large Power Electronics
- The technique of power conversion

Power Electronics is a port of application electronics or industrial electronics

Classify types of converters following the purpose:
- AC --> DC: Rectification
- AC --> AC: Converter with AC voltage, variable frequency drive (VFD)
- DC --> DC: Converter with DC voltage
- DC --> AC: Counter-flow / backflow / reverse flow

Converter = Power Electronic Circuit + Controller
