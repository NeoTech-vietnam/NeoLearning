### Privacy and Security

Many of the devices associated with the IoT have the capacity to gather large volumes of personal data.

This data is then subject to the possibility of analysis, with associated risks of misinterpretation impacting on privacy there is an increasing burden on system designers to place privacy at the core of their design process within the context each of the Internet of Things, Cyber-Physical Systems and Big Data, and that this must be reflected in the desing process itself and the methods and tools to support this.

### Sustainability

A recognized need to move towards more sustanable forms of society-centered around the individual and their needs and structured around the effective management and use of all available resources.

Concepts such as those of the smart home and the smart city where information is used to manage daily activities.

Creation of sustainable societies with individuals and the core to address issues such as ageing populations, resource availability and management, climate change and resilience.

### Education

Mechatronices education has always faced the challenge of balancing appropriate levels of technical content with the understanding of the requirements for integration across the core disciplines of mechanical engirneering, electronics and information technology.

**Changes in delivery**
- Massive On-line Open Courses (MOOCS).
- Tutorial and workshop based learning support.
- Blended learning.
- Impact of social media on learning.

**Structural Issues**
- Distributed learning resources.
- Time value of content.
- Collaborative working
