Answers are illustrative; accept other defensible decisions when assumptions and evidence limits are explicit.

## A. Product and learning decisions

1. Interview intended users about when they move between hosts, what state should follow, current alternatives, and willingness to carry/pay for a core. Test a removable mock-up and compare phone/cloud account sync and a conventional clock. Do not infer demand from enthusiasm alone.
2. Owner: choose target user/priorities and acceptable privacy/support promise; approve budget and continue/pivot/stop. Specialist review: lithium charging/protection; RF/antenna or production EMC. Owner remains accountable for accepting evidence and risk disposition.
3. Claim five observed course folders and propose bridges to product competencies. Do not claim lecture content, dates, credits, teaching sequence, or outcomes without readable primary evidence.
4. 720 h ÷ 6 h/week = **120 active weeks**. Exams, employment, procurement, breaks, and gate failures alter elapsed time; the calculation is not a calendar promise.

## D. Data rates and interface feasibility

10. 16,000 samples/s × 16 bits/sample = **256,000 bit/s = 256 kbit/s**, before framing/protocol overhead.
11. Frame: 320 × 240 × 2 = **153,600 bytes**. Camera: 153,600 × 10 × 8 = 12,288,000 bit/s = **12.288 Mbit/s**. Combined with PCM: 12.288 + 0.256 = **12.544 Mbit/s**, before overhead.
12. No. Need an actual interface/protocol design, sustained measured goodput, burst/buffering, overhead, latency, error behavior, power/grounding and contact integrity. Contact count alone proves none of these.
